## 3. Viewing Your Schedule

### 3.1 Accessing Your Schedule
1. Click "Schedule" in the navigation menu
2. You will see your personal schedule in calendar view:
   ![My Schedule](placeholder-for-my-schedule.png)
   *Take screenshot of your schedule view*
3. Use the date navigator at the top to move between weeks/months
4. Switch between "Week View", "Day View", and "List View" using the buttons

### 3.2 Understanding Schedule Colors
- **Blue Shifts**: Confirmed shifts you are assigned to
- **Green Shifts**: Open shifts you can volunteer for
- **Gray Shifts**: Shifts you are not assigned to
- **Red Shifts**: Shifts with scheduling conflicts

### 3.3 Viewing Shift Details
1. Click on any shift in your calendar
2. A shift detail panel will appear showing:
   - Shift title and time
   - Location/Department
   - Assigned employees
   - Your assignment status
   - Any notes or special instructions
3. Click "Close" or outside the panel to dismiss

### 3.4 Volunteering for Open Shifts
1. Find a green shift (open shift) you're interested in
2. Click on the shift to open the details
3. Click the "Volunteer for this Shift" button
4. Confirm your volunteer request in the popup
5. You will receive a notification when your request is approved/declined

## 4. Managing Availability

### 4.1 Submitting Your Availability
1. Click "Availability" in the navigation menu
2. You will see the availability calendar:
   ![Availability Calendar](placeholder-for-availability-calendar.png)
   *Take screenshot of the availability calendar*
3. The calendar shows date cells where you can indicate your availability
4. Click on a date cell to open the availability form for that day
5. Select your availability status:
   - **Available** - You can work any shift
   - **Unavailable** - You cannot work any shifts
   - **Preferred** - You prefer to work but can be flexible
   - **No Preference** - You have no preference (default)
6. Optionally, add notes about your availability (e.g., "Available after 2 PM")
7. Click "Save Availability"
8. Repeat for each date you wish to update

### 4.2 Bulk Availability Options
1. Use the "Fill Week" or "Fill Month" buttons to apply the same availability to multiple days
2. Select the date range
3. Choose your availability status
4. Click "Apply"

### 4.3 Viewing Team Availability
1. Toggle the "Team View" switch to see when your teammates are available
2. This helps you coordinate shift swaps or coverage
3. Team availability shows as color-coded overlays on the calendar

## 5. Requesting Time Off

### 5.1 Submitting a Time Off Request
1. Click "Time Off" in the navigation menu
2. Click the "+ Request Time Off" button
3. Fill in the request form:
   - **Request Type**: Vacation, Sick Leave, Personal, etc.
   - **Start Date**: First day of time off
   - **End Date**: Last day of time off (same as start for single day)
   - **Hours per Day**: Number of hours you normally work each day
   - **Reason**: Brief explanation (required for some request types)
   - **Attachment**: Optional - upload doctor's note or other documentation
4. Click "Submit Request"
   ![Time Off Request](placeholder-for-time-off-request.png)
   *Take screenshot of the time off request form*

### 5.2 Viewing Request Status
1. On the Time Off page, you'll see a list of all your requests
2. Each request shows:
   - Dates requested
   - Type of leave
   - Status (Pending, Approved, Denied, Cancelled)
   - Date submitted
3. Click on a request to view details or add comments
4. You can cancel a pending request by clicking the "Cancel" button

### 5.3 Receiving Notifications
1. You will receive email notifications when:
   - Your time off request is submitted
   - Your request is approved or denied
   - Additional information is needed
2. In-app notifications will also appear in your dashboard

## 6. Viewing Pay Stub & Reports

### 6.1 Accessing Pay Information
1. Click "Pay Stub" in the navigation menu
2. Select the pay period you wish to view from the dropdown
3. Your pay stub will display:
   - Regular hours worked
   - Overtime hours
   - Holiday hours
   - Total earnings
   - Deductions (taxes, benefits, etc.)
   - Net pay
4. Click "Download PDF" to save or print your pay stub
   ![Pay Stub](placeholder-for-pay-stub.png)
   *Take screenshot of the pay stub view*

### 6.2 Viewing Work Reports
1. Click "Reports" in the navigation menu (if available)
2. Select from available reports:
   - Hours Worked Summary
   - Shift History
   - Overtime Report
   - Availability Compliance
3. Choose date range and click "Generate Report"
4. Export options: PDF, Excel, Print

## 7. Profile Management

### 7.1 Updating Your Profile
1. Click "Profile" in the navigation menu
2. You will see your profile information:
   - Personal Information (name, contact details)
   - Employment Information (hire date, department, position)
   - Skills & Certifications
   - Emergency Contacts
3. Click the "Edit Profile" button to make changes
4. Update any information that has changed
5. Click "Save Changes"

### 7.2 Changing Your Password
1. On the Profile page, click the "Change Password" button
2. Enter your current password
3. Enter your new password
4. Confirm your new password
5. Click "Save Changes"

### 7.3 Notification Preferences
1. On the Profile page, scroll to "Notification Settings"
2. Toggle switches for:
   - Email notifications for schedule changes
   - Email notifications for time off approvals
   - Email notifications for shift reminders
   - In-app notifications
   - SMS notifications (if available)
3. Click "Save Preferences"

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues
- **Cannot Login**: Verify credentials, ensure Caps Lock is off, use "Forgot Password" link if available
- **Schedule Not Loading**: Refresh the browser, clear cache, check internet connection
- **Unable to Submit Availability**: Ensure you're looking at the correct date range, contact administrator if issue persists
- **Time Off Request Denied**: Check with your supervisor for reasons and resubmit if appropriate

### Getting Help
- Refer to the FAQ section in the Help menu
- Contact your supervisor or department manager
- For technical issues, contact IT support at support@company.com
- For schedule-related questions, contact the scheduling administrator
